## AI is smart enough already.<br>We make it good to use.

We explore new ways to live and work with intelligence.

### Two things in the making

**[Mochi](https://mochi.host/)** — A cat that lives in your Feishu group and
reads your GitHub. Answers with sources, turns agreed decisions into Issues,
and follows up on progress. Runs on your Mac, with your own ChatGPT.

**[Galta](https://galta.art/en/)** — An AI canvas that lives in your browser.
Collect web references, then create images, films, and interactive pages
beside them. Your work and history stay local. Early access applications are open,
and the [source](https://github.com/aha-labs-hq/galta) is public.
